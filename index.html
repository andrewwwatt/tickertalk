BULLISHReview #1
Rainbow Chicken (RBO)
Finalisation announcement regarding the declaration of a special dividend
← Previous: BULLISH on 13 Aug
Rainbow Chicken finalising a special dividend — cash returned to shareholders after what appears to be a strong operational period. The poultry giant is benefiting from industry consolidation and reduced import competition.

Rainbow Chicken is returning cash to shareholders via a special dividend, which is almost always a bullish signal from management — they have cash they don't need for operations or growth. The SA poultry sector has been on a remarkable run: Astral Foods' recent results showed the best margins in years, and Rainbow (listed separately after the RCL demerger) is riding the same wave.

Industry dynamics are favourable: reduced import competition from the US/EU anti-dumping duties, lower feed costs (maize prices off their peaks), and industry consolidation that's given the big producers pricing power. A special dividend says management thinks the cycle has legs and the balance sheet can support both payouts and capex. The devil's in the quantum, but the signal is clear. BULLISH — when poultry companies pay specials, the cycle is working.
