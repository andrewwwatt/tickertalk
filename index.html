York Timber Holdings (YRK)
Trading statement and trading update, including notification of the unbundling of certain assets
Trading statement combined with asset unbundling notification. York Timber is restructuring its asset base — separating operating assets from non-core holdings.

York Timber dropped a dual announcement yesterday: a trading statement and an unbundling notification. The trading statement will clarify the earnings trajectory for the forestry and timber processing group, but the unbundling is the real strategic signal. Separating assets — likely the forestry plantations from the processing operations — is a value-unlocking exercise that could surface hidden NAV. Forestry stocks trade at a discount to their underlying land and timber asset values because the market struggles to value the biological asset base. An unbundling could crystallise that value. York Timber has been restructuring for years and the balance sheet has been under pressure. The full SENS text will tell us whether this is a genuine value unlock or a necessity-driven spin-off. NEUTRAL — the concept is right but execution risk is high in a forestry cycle.
