Business Performance Metrics For The Three Months Ended 30 September 2026
← Previous: NEUTRAL on 30 Sep
Sasol's best quarter in years: mining production hit a 6-year high, ORYX GTL restarted after the Middle East shutdown, and management is on track within guidance. NERSA approved the gas price for FY27 and part of FY28.

Sasol's first quarter reads like a company that has finally stopped stepping on its own feet. Mining delivered its best quarterly production in six years, ORYX GTL is back online after the Middle East conflict forced it to close in March, and liquid fuels volumes grew as customers returned and inventories were drawn down. Management says it remains on track within market guidance, with conflict-driven pricing staying supportive across the portfolio. There's still a bump in the road — Natref's desulphuriser had a process safety incident and only restarts at end-October, and Chemicals Africa revenue slipped on lower volumes — but the direction of travel is finally forward.

The quiet wins matter as much as the big ones: NERSA approved Sasol's maximum gas price for FY27 and part of FY28, giving pricing certainty; the nitrates business is being sold to Enaex; and sustainable aviation fuel production starts at Natref next quarter. At R266.69 with a forward PE under 8, the market is still pricing in the old Sasol. This is the new-ish Sasol, and it's working. BULLISH.
