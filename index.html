Full interims land 10 November. At 15x trailing earnings with a 25% ROE, this remains one of the JSE's most dependable compounders. Nothing to dislike here.

Southern Palladium's updated Bengwenyama PFS lifted post-tax NPV 129% to US$1.96bn with a 39.1% IRR, and the 30-year mining right is now in hand — a junior project actually de-risking.

Southern Palladium is small, speculative and dual-listed down under, but this quarterly just got a whole lot more interesting. The updated PFS lifted post-tax NPV 129% to US$1.96bn, IRR to 39.1% and payback to 2.8 years, while chromite recovery estimates tripled versus the old PFS — chrome as a co-product sweetens the PGM story. Add the 30-year mining right granted in August and you have a junior that's genuinely de-risking.

This is still a development-stage stock with no earnings yet, so the PE stays blank. But the board has already approved early box-cut and decline development, and the balance sheet is fully funded to complete the DFS. If Bengwenyama is the tier-1 PGM project the numbers suggest, R18.39 looks cheap. If not, you own a risky explorer. Speculative buy — sized as such.
