🟢 ARL — Astral Foods dropped a trading statement that looks like a misprint: EPS expected 90-100% higher, driven by softer maize and soya prices slashing feed costs. Poultry farming efficiencies and a shift into higher-margin segments add operational tailwinds. Results drop 23 November — worth the wait.

🟡 DCP — Dis-Chem grew revenue 8% to R23bn on the Better Rewards loyalty programme, but EPS fell 13.3% as the cost of rewarding customers compressed margins. The dividend was trimmed 13% to 25.62c. A good business going through a strategic margin reset.

🟢 ADR — Adcorp's revenue slipped 2.1% but operating profit jumped 24.9% and EPS rose 14.3%. The interim dividend is up 14.9% to 28.46c. Quiet turnaround on a PE of 4.7x — nobody read past the top line.

🟡 ANH — AB InBev grew Q3 revenue 4.9% and underlying profit 21.6% to US$2.4bn. Corona grew 20% outside its home market. But the JSE-listed share dropped 7% on a 34bp margin contraction — market is punishing staples.

🟢 DTC — Datatec grew gross invoiced income 18% and adjusted EBITDA 16.6%. The ICT group trades on 10x trailing PE with a 17.9% ROE and a forward multiple of 7.1x. Cheap, growing, ignored.

🟢 NPH — Northam's strategic process has attracted "significant interest" from SA and multinational corporations, with proposals arriving ahead of the 1 Dec deadline. Ghost Mail also flagged a credit rating upgrade. The share jumped 5.8%.

🔴 MTU — Mantengu is selling its silicon carbide subsidiary for exactly R1,001 after Eskom refused a new tariff. Sublime has generated no income since June 2025. R0.18 share, 50% dilution in a year, 85% negative ROE. House-cleaning, not a turnaround.
