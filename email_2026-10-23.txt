<h3 style="color: #1a1a2e; margin-top: 24px;">The quick version</h3>


<p>🟡 <strong>PIK</strong> — The loss is shrinking (EPS loss -40.52c to -33.77c vs -67.53c) and Pick n Pay made peace with SACCAWU, formally withdrawing the Section 189A retrenchment process. Still loss-making, but the olive branch matters — real proof lands Monday.</p>

<p>🟢 <strong>CLS</strong> — First time over R50bn turnover, HEPS +4.4%, dividend up 4.4% to 925c, ROE 48.7%. UPD was the laggard and inventory is up 11%, but this is a compounding machine doing compounder things in a squeezed market. Share dipped 3.6% after a 17% monthly run — profit-taking, not panic.</p>

<p>🟢 <strong>CNP</strong> — Revenue +37.5% on the MultiChoice consolidation, 2026 guidance intact, and a stadium's worth of new sports rights (UEFA clubs across 40+ countries from 2027, AFCON 2027). Like-for-like is flat — this is a scale story, and the market loved it, +8%.</p>

<p>🔴 <strong>AFT</strong> — "Toughest period since listing": headline earnings swung to a -58c loss, EPS down 97%, dividend cut to nothing. Iron ore profits collapsed from R445m to R8.6m. Balance sheet is sound; the earnings aren't. The 5.9% sell-off was honest.</p>

<p>🟢 <strong>SEA</strong> — Distribution up 8% to 44.92c, vacancy of just 1.1%, LTV down to 21.9%. The all-Western-Cape specialist keeps quietly compounding while the rest of property whines about empty buildings.</p>

<p>🟡 <strong>ZED</strong> — NAV fell 16% to R1.41, but the story is the cash: ~R1.4bn from the Zaad disposal expected by 30 November, most of it destined for shareholders, plus a sell-side process running on May Seed. Trading at a 25% discount to NAV while you wait.</p>

<p>🟢 <strong>4SI</strong> — Revenue +29% to R748m, HEPS +11% to 7.49c, trading under 7x earnings and below book. Margin dilution is the caveat and micro-cap risk is real — but the numbers quietly keep improving.</p>

<p>🟡 <strong>ACL</strong> — No binding offer, ministerial approval outstanding, "no certainty" — and the share still jumped 6.7% on IDC rescue hopes. Hope is being priced, not earnings. Own the caution, not the rumour.</p>
