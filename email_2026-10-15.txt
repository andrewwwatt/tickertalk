🟢 EQU — Equites hiked its interim distribution 5.1% to 72.57c, reaffirmed full-year guidance of 147.7–150.5c (5–7% growth), completed its UK exit and cut LTV from 35.1% to 28.1% — a clean, confident set of interims.

🔴 FBR — Famous Brands warns headline earnings will fall up to 13% (HEPS 252c–205c) and reported EPS by as much as 33%, dragged down by a R40.9m goodwill impairment on its Botswana Retail Group stake as it cuts from 51% to 30%.

🟢 FTA — Fairvest launched an accelerated book build to raise approximately R500m in new B shares via Java Capital, earmarked to fund 'strategic retail acquisitions under negotiation'.

🟢 KST — PSG Financial Services delivered a 5% rise in recurring headline earnings to 60.9c, a 10% dividend hike to 22c, 15% growth in assets under management to R594.1bn and a 26.5% ROE — a quietly brilliant set of numbers.

🟡 MNP — Mondi's Q3 underlying EBITDA rose to €196m from €167m in Q2 (helped by a zero forestry fair value swing vs a -€43m loss), but ex-forestry earnings fell sequentially as energy and wood costs bit; the market knocked the shares ~10%.

🟢 SRE — Fitch reaffirmed Sirius' BBB investment grade rating with a stable outlook, citing ~7x EBITDA interest cover, disciplined leverage, and growing defence-related exposure in Germany worth around €200m.

🟢 THA — Tharisa signed a binding multi-year deal for Valterra Platinum to take 50% of Tharisa Mine's PGM concentrate from January 2027 — replacing Sibanye-Stillwater as offtake partner — plus a fine-chrome recovery collaboration using its Vulcan X technology.
